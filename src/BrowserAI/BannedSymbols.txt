T:System.Console;stdout is the JSON-RPC protocol channel. Use StdioChannel for the wire and ILogger for diagnostics. Console.Out writes CP437 and CRLF, which corrupts a frame on the first non-ASCII byte; Console.In steals bytes the client sent as JSON-RPC and hangs forever on a server with no keyboard.
T:System.Diagnostics.Debug;Debug.Assert raises a modal dialog on a machine with nobody at it, which is an invisible hang on a background MCP server. Throw, or log and carry on.
