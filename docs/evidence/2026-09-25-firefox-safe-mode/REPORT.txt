Q302 - the stuck Firefox launch. Research report, 2026-09-25 (runs 2026-09-24T23:01Z to 2026-09-25T00:02Z).
Read-only against the repository; all scratch under .work/firefox-launch/. Firefox 1549 (156.0), playwright-core 1.64.0-alpha-1789764292000, node v24.21.0 from payload/.

=====================================================================
ANSWER
=====================================================================
The stuck launch is Firefox starting in SAFE MODE. If Shift is down (and Ctrl/Alt are not) at the moment
Firefox's browser process starts, it enters safe mode unless the environment variable MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY
is set. In safe mode, BrowserGlue opens a modal "Troubleshoot Mode" window before any browser window. In
headless mode nobody can see or dismiss that window, so the first browser window never opens, the
"browser-idle-startup-tasks-finished" notification never fires, juggler never answers Browser.enable, and
Playwright gives up at its own 180 s launch timeout. The suite then reports
"async initializeServer: Timeout 180000ms exceeded".
It is not contention, not memory pressure, not the stray sweep, and not a profile lock. Serializing the Firefox
tests would not help. The fix is one line: set MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY=1 in ChildEnvironment.Forced.
It also affects real users, not just the suite: anyone holding Shift at the instant a Firefox session starts gets a
180 s failure.

=====================================================================
EVIDENCE CHAIN (each link measured or read, with where)
=====================================================================
1. Reproduced on an idle machine, one launch at a time, no suite running:
   4 stuck in 133 serial launches without the override (smoke 1/2, serial1 2/30, A/B control 1/101).
   smoke-0002 ran to the full budget: 180,009.3 ms, "browserType.launchPersistentContext: Timeout 180000ms
   exceeded." Its call log is line for line the suite's PasswordPromptTests failure (.work/suite/p1b-bash-1.log):
   "Juggler listening to the pipe", "RenderCompositorSWGL failed mapping default framebuffer, no dt",
   "Helper.js, line 82 ... removeProgressListener", and then nothing.
2. Those three log lines are noise. They appear in 40/40 healthy launches and 3/3 stuck ones
   (runs/*/probe.log). What separates the two groups:
   - Browser.enable (id 1): answered in 40/40 healthy launches and never answered in any stuck one. In the
     stuck ones, requests 2 to 12 were answered in 20 to 90 ms (runs/smoke/smoke-0002/probe.log).
   - Browser.attachedToTarget and the delayed-startup output ("[ERROR shell_windows::limited_access_features]"):
     40/40 healthy, 0/3 stuck.
   - sessionCheckpoints.json stops at {"profile-after-change":true,"final-ui-startup":true}. Healthy launches
     add "sessionstore-windows-restored".
3. The stuck browser is idle, not deadlocked. Browser pid 106416 used +0 ms CPU over 2 s. Its main thread
   (tid 78248, which owns MozillaHiddenWindowClass / nsAppShell:EventWindowClass) is in Wait:UserRequest and
   answered WM_NULL in 0 ms (runs/serial1/serial1-0003/inspect-1-2-106416.txt). The pid Playwright spawns is
   Firefox's launcher (1 thread, 0 ms CPU). The browser is the launcher's child.
4. Every stuck browser was in safe mode. Its tab content process was started with "-safeMode": 3 of 3
   organic stalls inspected (serial1-0003, serial1-0016 inspect files, ab1-0109 probe.suspect-tree.txt) and
   5 of 5 forced stalls. 0 of 287 healthy trees read had it.
   Firefox passes -safeMode to content processes exactly when the parent's gSafeMode is set:
   mozilla-firefox/firefox dom/ipc/ContentParent.cpp "geckoargs::sSafeMode.Put(gSafeMode, extraArgs)", and
   toolkit/xre/GeckoArgs.h sSafeMode{"-safeMode"}.
5. Forcing safe mode through Firefox's own switch reproduces the exact signature 5 of 5, deterministically,
   with no keyboard involved: MOZ_SAFE_MODE_RESTART=1 gave 3/3 stuck (runs/forced1). All had Browser.enable
   unanswered, a -safeMode content process, and a 21-file profile of about 850 KB at 30 s, the same as the
   organic stalls at 30 s (serial1-0003 850,726 B, ab1-0109 850,019 B). Adding MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY=1 on
   top still gave 2/2 stuck (runs/forced2), so the override covers the key trigger only, as the source says.
6. The override removed every stall where it was applied: 0 of 101 serial launches (the A/B treatment arm,
   interleaved launch by launch with the control) and 0 of 54 in parallel batches. Stated with its limit: the
   interleaved A/B alone is 1/101 against 0/101, which by itself is not significant, because the trigger
   depends on keyboard state that nothing here controls. The case rests on links 4, 5 and 7 together.
7. The source chain, read from the shipped build:
   - toolkit/xre/SafeMode.h (mozilla-firefox/firefox @3682546) IsSafeModeRequested: on Windows,
     GetKeyState(VK_SHIFT) down with Ctrl and Alt up enters safe mode, unless EnvHasValue("MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY").
     The same function returns true for MOZ_SAFE_MODE_RESTART. The DisableSafeMode registry policy is the only
     other off switch, and it is machine or user wide.
   - nsAppRunner.cpp: "IsSafeModeRequested(gArgc, gArgv)" (the key check is on) sets gSafeMode in the browser
     process. browser/app/winlauncher/LauncherProcessWin.cpp passes SafeModeFlag::NoKeyPressCheck, so the
     launcher never checks the key.
   - This build carries the switches: "MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY" occurs 1x in xul.dll and 1x in firefox.exe of
     firefox-1549 (grep -a), and MOZ_SAFE_MODE_RESTART 3x and 2x.
   - browser/omni.ja modules/BrowserGlue.sys.mjs:205-206: final-ui-startup calls _beforeUIStartup. At :395-407,
     "before the first window is opened", `if (Services.appinfo.inSafeMode) Services.ww.openWindow(null,
     "chrome://browser/content/safeMode.xhtml", "_blank", "chrome,centerscreen,modal,resizable=no", null)`.
     Nothing checks for headless mode.
   - The observer order explains why "Juggler listening" still prints: xpcom/ds/nsObserverList.h says observers
     are notified "in last-added-first order". Juggler registers for final-ui-startup later, at
     command-line-startup, so it runs first.
   - omni.ja chrome/juggler/content/components/Juggler.js:47-48 and :90-91: the startup promise resolves only on
     "browser-idle-startup-tasks-finished", or immediately under -silent at :83-87, which Playwright passes only
     for non-persistent launches. :149 prints "Juggler listening to the pipe".
     protocol/BrowserHandler.js:30-33: Browser.enable awaits that promise.
   - browser/omni.ja chrome/browser/content/browser/browser-init.js:411-412 waits for "MozAfterPaint" before it
     runs _delayedStartup. :728-737 then runs promiseAllWindowsRestored, the idle tasks, and
     "browser-delayed-startup-finished". :1052 fires "browser-idle-startup-tasks-finished".
   - playwright-core lib/coreBundle.js:4684 DEFAULT_PLAYWRIGHT_LAUNCH_TIMEOUT = 3*60*1e3, applied by
     _launchTimeout at :58317-58325. FFBrowser.connect sends Browser.enable at :46316-46340. Firefox's
     waitForReadyState resolves on "Juggler listening" at :46701-46708, which is why the launch passes that stage.
8. The suite's own records fit. In p1b-ps-3 (FirefoxSessionTests, 3m01.8s) the stuck Firefox, pid 79400, was
   alive and holding its profile lock at 21:34:13, 21:34:16 and 21:34:21Z (Sweep[8] records from pids
   28092/103868/79000 in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BrowserAI\logs\browserai-20260924-001.log). Its error text was not
   kept, so it is classified by duration and liveness alone. kb row 34's failed cost-ratio round had its
   profile stall at 1,159,208 B across 25 files. smoke-0002's profile at 180 s was 1,159,281 B across 25 files.
   Healthy profiles have 56 to 64 files and 21.3 to 36.3 MB, and always include cert9.db, key4.db and
   places.sqlite. No stuck profile has any of the three.

=====================================================================
HYPOTHESES, RANKED, WITH EVIDENCE FOR AND AGAINST
=====================================================================
1. Safe mode from a held Shift key at browser-process start: ESTABLISHED (see chain above).
   Against: nothing observed contradicts it. The keyboard state itself was not observed: this rig does not read
   input. Whether a person or some software held Shift at those instants is therefore not measured. The observed
   rate moved with time: 3 of the first 32 launches (23:01 to 23:14Z), then 1 of the next 200. That fits a trigger
   tied to human activity, but proves nothing about it.
2. Contention between parallel Firefox launches: RULED OUT as the cause.
   - Batches of 3 without the override: 0 stuck in 30.
   - Batches of 3 and 6 with it: 0 stuck in 54.
   - Stalls occur with one launch at a time on an idle machine.
   Against ruling it out: nothing. Concurrency only slows launches (see the tables).
3. Memory or commit pressure: RULED OUT as the cause of this signature.
   - A commit limit on Firefox's own job fails FAST, not stuck: 450 MB (2/2) and 300 MB (2/2) failed in 3.6 to
     13.1 s. The logs show "out of memory" and the browser exiting 0xC0000005, and Playwright reports "Target page,
     context or browser has been closed".
   - Commit percentage is not physical pressure on this machine: 128 GB RAM and a 10 GB pagefile. At 73.4%
     commit (2026-09-24 ~23:0xZ), 73.5 GB was available and the pagefile held 137 MB in use. One game process
     alone was 26.2 GB private with 8.4 GB resident.
   - The TIGHT label on both failing runs sits 0.2 to 0.7 points above HEALTHY runs that passed (74.8%, 74.4%).
   Against: available physical memory at the two failure times was not recorded by anything, so it was not read.
4. First start in a fresh profile under CPU starvation: RULED OUT as the cause. A job CPU cap of 1% of the
   machine (about 0.32 logical CPU) slowed launch to 9.4 s against 2.4 s at p50, and 2 of 2 completed.
5. The stray sweep killing a browser mid-launch: RULED OUT. All 20 sweeps in the two failure windows logged
   terminated=0: 11 in 21:00 to 21:07Z and 9 in 21:33 to 21:40Z. A killed browser fails fast through Playwright's
   exit race (coreBundle.js:40592-40603) instead of timing out.
6. A profile lock: RULED OUT for both 2026-09-24 instances. "Juggler listening" comes after profile locking, and
   the front-door Firefox was attributed through its own lock. It remains a candidate for the 2026-09-22 row-38
   instance, which stopped before "Juggler listening" (see NOT ESTABLISHED).
7. Defender scanning a new profile: NOT NEEDED AND NOT SUPPORTED. The stuck browsers were idle, with 0 CPU and
   message pumps answering, not blocked in I/O. Real-time protection is on, and exclusions cannot be read
   without admin.
8. The Firefox content-process limit: RULED OUT. playwright.cfg sets dom.ipc.processCount 60000 and
   processPrelaunch off. Stuck trees had 3 content processes.
9. The GFX "no dt" error and the Helper.js removeProgressListener error: NOISE, present in 40/40 healthy launches.

DIRECTIONS FOR Q302
=====================================================================
1. (RECOMMENDED) Add ["MOZ_DISABLE_SAFE_MODE_KEY"] = "1" to ChildEnvironment.Forced
   (src/BrowserAI/Protocol/ChildEnvironment.cs:217-221).
   How it reaches Firefox: node gets it through ChildLaunch.cs:162-163, and Playwright hands process.env to
   Firefox (coreBundle.js:40539/40547). Juggler's amendEnvironment adds nothing on Windows (:46666).
   Coverage: the product and all four suite Firefox arms (ChildLaunch.Create in PasswordPromptTests.cs:230 and
   FirefoxTests.cs:484; Harness/JobObjectScope.cs:62 for containment; the published slice for
   FirefoxSessionTests, after a republish).
   Precedent: Mozilla sets the same variable in devtools/client/framework/browser-toolbox/Launcher.sys.mjs
   ("Disable safe mode for the new process in case this was opened via the keyboard shortcut"), as does
   mozilla/mozregression launchers.py.
   Cost: one line, a doc comment, a republish, kb and HAZARDS rows.
   Test:
   (a) ChildEnvironmentTests asserts Build() carries the pair. It goes red by deleting the line.
   (b) One live Firefox launch asserts that no content process carries -safeMode. The harness reads command lines
       already (Harness/ProcessCommandLine.cs). Reading the browser's environment block would need a new PEB
       reader. The detector's deterministic positive control is MOZ_SAFE_MODE_RESTART=1, 5/5 here.
   What a test cannot do: the behaviour under a real held Shift cannot be planted red in this suite without
   driving the machine's keyboard state. A behaviour-level red is therefore not available, and accepting a
   mechanism-level test would be a second named exception to the plant-it-red rule. That is the maintainer's call.
2. Detect and recover in the product. After launch, look for -safeMode or an unanswered Browser.enable, then
   refuse with a named error and relaunch. This covers every safe-mode trigger. Cost: new product code on the
   launch path.
   Test: deterministic, through a test-only MOZ_SAFE_MODE_RESTART seam. The other triggers cannot reach a child
   today: the environment is an allow-list (ChildEnvironment.cs:133-202) and the product writes the arguments.
   Heavier than needed on top of 1.
3. Shorter launch budget, through browser.launchOptions.timeout in the generated config. A stuck launch fails in,
   say, 60 s instead of 180 s, and the next call relaunches, because upstream clears the failed backend
   (coreBundle.js:73325-73328).
   Cost: one key. It treats the symptom, and it is a promptness bound in product code: a 1%-CPU Firefox took
   9.4 s here, and the suite has seen 6 s.
   Test: deterministic through the same seam. Not recommended alone.
4. Report upstream to microsoft/playwright with this reproduction. Juggler Firefox's amendEnvironment could set
   the variable, as the BiDi path already sets MOZ_CRASHREPORTER* (coreBundle.js:44593-44601). Link #40882
   ("newPage sometimes hangs indefinitely on Firefox", Windows 11), which was closed 2026-06-15 because nobody
   had a reproduction.
   Cost: one issue. It complements 1.
5. Serialize the Firefox tests: WOULD NOT HELP. The trigger is per launch and independent of concurrency
   (4/133 serial on an idle machine, 0/84 in batches). It costs suite wall clock and the race detection that
   SuiteParallelism.cs:26-32 keeps unbounded for.
RECOMMENDATION: 1, with 4 alongside.
