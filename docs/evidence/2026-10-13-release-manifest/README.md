## Two departures from the bytes as emitted, both of them the convention's

**(1) The release body is `.txt` and was emitted as `.md`**, because every `.md` in this tree
carries a two-line SPDX header, and the body's value is that it is byte for byte what was
published. The extension changed and not one byte did.

**(2) Three files were emitted with CRLF and are stored with LF**, because
[`.gitattributes`](../../../.gitattributes) normalises line endings for everything this
repository tracks. The LF form of `tests-BrowserAI.Tests.packages.lock.json` equals the committed
`tests/BrowserAI.Tests/packages.lock.json` at the tag, to the byte; `manifest.json` and
`payload.json` have no committed original, so their as-emitted digests are recorded here and
nowhere else.

| File | Emitted bytes | SHA-256 as emitted | Stored bytes | SHA-256 as stored |
|---|--:|---|--:|---|
| `manifest.json` | 4,201 | `d65d84874bdb4ad5505fc5af298b47e9c23a990d9d4811a29a66af69a5919b91` | 4,091 | `736f651b92feeb209bec6e704446b06ab4189ec6bc5678605041ccc54dae666e` |
| `payload.json` | 1,131 | `344b0e1bce2cff484cdec5770a0846d919696c7f909c2dc2e63d7762bff75c9f` | 1,096 | `6f731aa6c11a06c96d4d9814f7dc355ac4e80388599148ee25986b9ba6e3207a` |
| `tests-BrowserAI.Tests.packages.lock.json` | 15,713 | `8c8bc8e911fac4df4f1dfa6baf2e8fcf6cb0f0830f026d71eb4e8d8798235c75` | 15,351 | `4b657e59014e0fc612dd07d6d316997571d195ca4144ef120df13fd6da7f2909` |

**The other seven files are byte-identical either way**: they were emitted with LF and are
stored with LF, and the six among them with a committed original, the two other lock files,
the payload's `package-lock.json` and `package.json`, `browsers.json` and `tool-verdicts.json`,
equal that file at the tag to the byte.

**How to re-establish it.** The emitted directory is regenerated by
`pwsh -File build/Write-ReleaseManifest.ps1` from the tree at the tag, or by
`build/New-Release.ps1`, which calls it. Comparing this copy against a fresh one means
comparing the LF forms, for the reason above.
