THE CORRECTION THIS FORCES. Google's representative said only which TERMS apply. The sentence
"so no redistribution is possible" is the QUESTIONER's, posted two minutes later, and nobody
from Google replied to it. So "a Google engineer ... reads those terms as forbidding
redistribution" attributes to Google a reading Google did not state. What Google stated is the
pointer; the forbidding is this repository's own reading of the document at the end of it.

THE TRAP BESIDE IT, worth recording because a search falls into it. GoogleChromeLabs/
chrome-for-testing carries an Apache-2.0 LICENSE file, and a web search for
"Chrome for Testing redistribute license" surfaces it as though it licensed the browser.
Bynens' first comment is the thing that rules that out in one sentence: it "applies to the
code in this repository", which is a dashboard and a JSON API.

WHERE THE POINTER LEADS, so the writer has the chain and not just its first link:
  https://www.google.com/chrome/terms/ -- "Google Chrome and ChromeOS Additional Terms of
  Service", Last modified 30 September 2025, fetched 2026-09-23. It states "Most source code
  for Chrome is available free of charge under open source software license agreements at
  chrome://credits" and refers the rest to the main Google Terms of Service.
  https://policies.google.com/terms -- Google Terms of Service, Effective July 30, 2026,

     "You may not copy, modify, distribute, sell, or lease any part of our services or software."

NOT ESTABLISHABLE HERE, and this is the honest verdict on the load-bearing half. Whether those
two documents forbid redistributing the Chrome for Testing binaries this product provisions is
a legal reading, and it is the same class of question this repository already refuses to answer
for the CsWin32 metadata licence -- nobody here may supply one. What WOULD establish it: a
written answer from Google to that question, or counsel's opinion. What has changed today is
that the evidence is no longer a half-remembered remark by an unnamed person -- it is a dated,
attributable, quotable comment, and it says less than the repository has been saying it says.
