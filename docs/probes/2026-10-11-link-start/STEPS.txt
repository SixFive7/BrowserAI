THE 55 a MEASUREMENT: about three minutes in Firefox

The rig is started for you before step 1 and taken down after you say done. It
records the times, which process opened which window, and whether that window
came to the front, so you only need to watch and, if you like, note what you saw.

Each link opens either a folder window named Probe-<letter>-<page>, or Windows'
"Browse For Folder" picker. Close each folder window (X) and cancel each picker
before the next step.

STEPS 1 TO 3 ANSWER THE MAIN QUESTIONS

1. In Firefox, open  http://127.0.0.1:53117/t1/   (paste it into the address bar).
   Click link A.
   Firefox asks: "Allow this site to open the browserai-probe link with BrowserAI
   link probe?". Tick "Always allow http://127.0.0.1:53117 to open browserai-probe
   links", then click "Open Link" (it can be clicked after about a second).
   A folder window Probe-A-p1 opens. Note: in front of Firefox, or behind it
   (with a flashing taskbar button)? Close it.

2. Open  http://127.0.0.1:53118/t1/   (the same page on another port, as the
   dashboard gets each time it opens).
   Click link A. Note: does Firefox ask again? If it asks, tick "Always allow"
   and click "Open Link".
   Probe-A-p2: in front or behind? Close it.

3. Go back to the first tab (port 53117). Click link C.
   A "Browse For Folder" picker opens: in front or behind? Click Cancel.

STEPS 4 TO 7 DECIDE BETWEEN THE TWO WAYS TO BUILD IT (about one more minute)

4. Same tab. Click link E. This is what the dashboard does today: Probe-E-p1 is
   expected to open behind Firefox, with a flashing taskbar button. Close it.

5. Click link B. Probe-B-p1: in front or behind? Close it.

6. Click link D. A picker: in front or behind? Cancel.

7. Open  http://127.0.0.1:53117/t2/   (the first port again, with another path,
   as a new dashboard secret on a port that was kept would look).
   Click link A. Does Firefox ask? Probe-A-p1b: in front or behind? Close it.

ONLY IF YOU ALSO USE CHROME (optional)

8. In Chrome, open  http://127.0.0.1:53117/t1/  and click A. Chrome asks to open
   "BrowserAI link probe"; tick its "Always allow" box and open. In front or
   behind? Close it.
9. In Chrome, open  http://127.0.0.1:53118/t1/  and click A. Does it ask again?
   In front or behind? Close it.

IF FIREFOX ASKS SOMETHING ELSE

- If Firefox offers "Choose an application" instead of "Open Link", pick
  "BrowserAI link probe" and tick "Remember". Say so when you report.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE

- Optional, 20 seconds: on each probe tab, click the site information icon left of
  the address, and remove "Open applications" with its X. That forgets the two
  "Always allow" answers. Left in place they are harmless: the scheme is removed
  in the cleanup, so they allow nothing.
- Close the probe tabs and say "done", with your notes if you made any,
  for example: "1 front, 2 asked, front, 3 front, 4 behind, 5 front, 6 front,
  7 no ask, front".
